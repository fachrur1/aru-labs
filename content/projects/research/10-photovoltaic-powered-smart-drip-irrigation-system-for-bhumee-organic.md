Problem:

Bhumee Organic, an organic farming cooperative in Yogyakarta, relied on conventional irrigation methods that resulted in excessive water consumption, inefficient labor utilization, and difficulties in maintaining optimal soil moisture levels for dragon fruit cultivation.

Approach / Process:

Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to design a photovoltaic-powered smart drip irrigation system for a 500 m² agricultural area. Contributed to the electrical system design and IoT architecture, including soil moisture monitoring, automated irrigation control, water level management, and remote system monitoring. The system integrated solar panels, energy storage, ESP32-based controllers, soil moisture sensors, pumps, and cloud-based monitoring to support precision agriculture practices.

Outcome:

Developed a sustainable irrigation solution capable of optimizing water usage, reducing manual labor requirements, and supporting data-driven irrigation management. The project demonstrated how renewable energy and IoT technologies can be integrated to improve agricultural efficiency and sustainability in small-scale farming operations.

Key Learnings:

- Solar-powered system design and integration.

- IoT architecture for agricultural automation.

- Sensor-based monitoring and control systems.

- Sustainable engineering for real-world community applications.

- Cross-disciplinary collaboration between engineering and agriculture.
