## Problem
Achieving Indonesia's 2060 energy transition target requires region-specific renewable energy planning based on accurate resource assessments and long-term energy demand projections. However, the renewable energy potential of Kulon Progo Regency has not been comprehensively evaluated through an integrated framework that combines spatial suitability analysis, energy generation modeling, and energy transition scenarios.

## Approach
Conducting a comprehensive assessment of renewable energy resources in Kulon Progo Regency through the integration of GIS-based spatial analysis, renewable energy simulation, and energy system modeling. The study evaluates the technical potential of solar, wind, hydropower, and biomass resources while considering geographical, environmental, infrastructure, and land-use constraints.

Renewable energy generation potential is simulated using the NREL System Advisor Model (SAM), while future energy transition pathways are analyzed using the Long-range Energy Alternatives Planning System (LEAP). The Analytic Network Process (ANP) is applied to prioritize renewable energy technologies and determine the most suitable energy mix under the 2060 transition scenario.

## Outcome
Developing a data-driven renewable energy transition roadmap that identifies suitable development zones, estimates renewable energy generation potential, and provides strategic recommendations for achieving a sustainable and resilient regional energy mix in Kulon Progo Regency.
