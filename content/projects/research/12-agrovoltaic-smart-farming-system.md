Problem:

Agricultural operations in rural communities often face increasing energy costs and limited access to sustainable energy solutions. At the same time, farmers need to maintain productivity while adapting to climate change and improving resource efficiency. Integrating solar energy generation with agricultural activities presents an opportunity to address both challenges simultaneously.

Action:

Supported the field implementation of an agrovoltaic system that combines solar photovoltaic (PV) technology with agricultural land use. Assisted in the installation of PV support structures, cable routing, equipment assembly, and site preparation activities. Collaborated with researchers, engineers, and local stakeholders during the deployment of renewable energy infrastructure for smart farming applications.

Outcome:

Successfully contributed to the construction and deployment phase of the agrovoltaic system by supporting on-site installation activities and infrastructure preparation. The project established the foundation for integrating renewable energy technologies into community-based agricultural operations.

Key Learnings:

- Solar photovoltaic (PV) installation practices.

- Renewable energy system deployment.

- Field engineering and construction workflows.

- Electrical installation and equipment assembly.

- Collaboration within multidisciplinary research teams.

- Community-based renewable energy implementation.
