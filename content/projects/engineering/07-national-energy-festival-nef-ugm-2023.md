Problem:

As one of UGM's major energy-themed events, the National Energy Festival required a cohesive visual identity and engaging event atmosphere to effectively communicate its themes and enhance participant experience. The organizing committee also needed reliable media coverage to support event documentation and livestreaming activities.

Approach / Process:

Contributed to both the creative and media production aspects of the event. Designed and developed a 3D mascot to strengthen the festival's visual identity, created graphic assets for event decorations and supporting materials, and collaborated with the creative team to ensure visual consistency throughout the venue. Additionally, operated the Sony HXR-NX2500 camera during livestream sessions, capturing event activities and supporting real-time broadcasting.

Outcome:

Delivered visual assets that contributed to a cohesive event branding experience and enhanced the overall atmosphere of the festival. Supported successful livestream operations and event documentation, helping extend audience engagement beyond on-site participants.

Key Learnings:

- Event branding and visual communication.

- 3D mascot development for large-scale events.

- Graphic design for environmental and decorative applications.

- Live event production and camera operation.

- Working across creative and technical roles within a multidisciplinary team.
