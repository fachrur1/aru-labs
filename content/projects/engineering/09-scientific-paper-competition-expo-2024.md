Award: 3rd Place Winner

Organizer: Universitas Lampung

Problem:

Despite the growing interest in microalgae-based biodiesel as a sustainable energy source, achieving economically viable production remains a significant challenge. Improvements in both biological engineering and cultivation systems are required to enhance biomass and lipid productivity.

Approach / Process:

Collaborated in the development of the research project "Bioengineering of Microalgae: Genome Editing of Chlamydomonas reinhardtii for Enhanced Biodiesel Productivity Through CRISPRi." Contributed by designing a photobioreactor prototype intended to support controlled microalgae cultivation and future implementation of genome-editing strategies. Developed conceptual designs, system visualizations, and engineering representations to communicate the integration of biotechnology and renewable energy applications.

Outcome:

The project was awarded 3rd Place at the Scientific Paper Competition EXPO 2024, recognizing its innovative approach to combining synthetic biology and sustainable energy technologies for future biodiesel production.

Key Learnings:

- Biofuel and renewable energy technologies.

- Photobioreactor engineering concepts.

- Scientific research communication.

- Translating research concepts into engineering prototypes.

- Interdisciplinary collaboration between biotechnology and engineering fields.
