Contributed to renewable energy and sustainability initiatives through interdisciplinary research and engineering projects focused on bioenergy, solar energy integration, IoT-based agriculture systems, and community-centered technology implementation.

Problem:

Many renewable energy and sustainable agriculture solutions remain difficult to implement due to technical complexity, limited accessibility, and the lack of integrated systems that connect research concepts with real-world applications.

Approach / Process:

Collaborated within the Research & Innovation Division to develop renewable energy and smart agriculture projects. Contributed to the design of a photobioreactor prototype for microalgae-based biodiesel research, while also participating in the engineering design of a photovoltaic-powered smart drip irrigation system integrating solar energy, IoT monitoring, and automated irrigation control for sustainable farming applications.

Outcome:

Produced technical designs, system architectures, and engineering concepts that supported both research and community-oriented sustainability initiatives. The photobioreactor project was further developed and presented in a national scientific paper competition, while the smart irrigation project provided a practical framework for renewable-energy-powered agricultural technology deployment.
