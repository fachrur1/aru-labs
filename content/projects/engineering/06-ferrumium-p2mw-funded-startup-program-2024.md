Problem:

As Ferrumium evolved from a student entrepreneurship project into a startup-oriented venture, the team faced challenges in standardizing product development, improving production workflows, and sustaining innovation while preparing for broader market opportunities.

Approach / Process:

Led the technical and product development aspects of the project as Chief Technology Officer. Oversaw design improvements, evaluated production methods, coordinated technical decision-making, and explored new product concepts derived from recycled lighter components. Collaborated with multidisciplinary team members to ensure product quality, consistency, and scalability.

Outcome:

Supported Ferrumium's progression into the P2MW entrepreneurship program, helping establish a more structured product development process and strengthening the project's foundation for future growth. Contributed to the technical strategy behind transforming recycled materials into a distinctive and marketable product line.

Key Learnings:

- Technical leadership within an early-stage venture.

- Product development strategy and innovation management.

- Balancing creativity, production feasibility, and business objectives.

- Building scalable workflows for product-based startups.
