Problem:

Following the rebranding of PPSMB Kesatria into Pionir Kesatria, the organization required updated visual assets that reflected its renewed identity while preserving the familiarity and recognition of the mascot "Nala."

Approach / Process:

Refined and enhanced the existing 3D mascot design to align with the new branding direction. Worked closely with the design team to improve visual presentation, optimize assets for different use cases, and ensure consistency across promotional and communication materials.

Outcome:

Successfully adapted the mascot for the new event identity, contributing to a cohesive branding experience across digital and promotional platforms. The updated asset supported the organization's transition while maintaining strong visual recognition among participants.

Key Learnings:

- Adapting existing design assets to evolving branding requirements.

- Balancing consistency and innovation during a rebranding process.

- Optimizing 3D assets for broader usability across multiple platforms.
