Problem:

PPSMB Kesatria required a distinctive visual identity that could engage incoming students and strengthen the event's branding. The mascot "Nala" needed to be transformed from a conceptual character into a high-quality 3D asset suitable for various promotional and digital media.

Approach / Process:

Developed the 3D model of "Nala" by translating design concepts into a detailed digital asset. Collaborated with the creative team throughout the modeling and refinement process to ensure visual consistency with the event's branding guidelines. Iteratively improved the model based on feedback and requirements for different media applications.

Outcome:

Delivered a production-ready 3D mascot asset that was utilized across promotional materials, digital content, and event branding. The mascot contributed to a stronger and more recognizable visual identity for PPSMB Kesatria 2023.

Key Learnings:

- Translating 2D concepts into production-ready 3D assets.

- Maintaining visual consistency across multiple media formats.

- Collaborating within a multidisciplinary creative team under tight deadlines.
